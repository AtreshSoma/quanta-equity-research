    Why QUANTA

    It does the checking and the arithmetic, shows every step, and leaves each decision with you.

    • Explains every call

      Each stock gets one Decision, with the reasons behind it and any rule that held it back.

    • Says why the others were left out

      A scan keeps the names it passes over, each with the one rule that kept it out.

    • Sizes a position to your capital

      Shares are worked out from your capital and the stop, so the possible loss is known first.

    • Looks at what you already own

      A sound stock can still be too large a part of your account. It tells you when.

    • Keeps an honest record

      Every call is recorded and measured against the market. Until a model passes its tests, its buy calls say so.

    • Runs on your computer

      Free and open source, with no account and no ads. Connected to Robinhood, it can read your holdings but never trade.

    Read-only, enforced in code

    What QUANTA may ask Robinhood's official MCP server, and what it refuses before any request leaves your computer.

    Refused, whatever the list says

    • place_equity_order
    • review_equity_order
    • any tool named place, cancel, submit, transfer, withdraw or deposit

    Order execution is switched off in the source, so turning it on would take a code change.

    • Your sign-in stays with RobinhoodYou sign in on Robinhood's own page. The access token is kept in memory only: never on disk, in the browser or in logs. Disconnect or restart QUANTA and it is gone.
    • Nothing is made upNo sample portfolio is ever shown in its place. Missing data is shown as a limitation, never as a view on the stock. Hide amounts removes dollar figures from the page.

    Honest about what it knows

    Every published call is recorded and later measured against the market. Until a model passes tests on past data, its buy calls say so.

    Using a call well

    • Read the reasons, not only the call.The Decision comes with why it was made, the main risk and the rule that held it back, if any.
    • Treat it as research, not an instruction.QUANTA does not know your goals or taxes. Check every price with your broker before you act.
    • Size from the stop.A share count is a risk budget for the loss you accept, not a forecast of the gain.
    • Watch the tag."Tested: no edge found" means the model was tested on past data and showed no reliable edge. "Model not yet validated" means it has not passed its tests yet.
    1. RecordedThe call, score, levels and evidence status, stored once and never edited.
    2. EnteredAt the next session's open, not at the price you saw.
    3. ClosedAt the close of its horizon's last session: about three months for a Research call.
    4. ComparedReturn over SPY, or over the Nifty 50 for NSE stocks.
    5. CountedOnly after measured calls on at least separate dates.

    Freshness
    Every Decision dates its price. An out-of-date price blocks new money.
    Where it runs
    On your computer at 127.0.0.1, with no QUANTA account. It refuses to serve other machines.
    Checkable
    Each call stores a fingerprint of the code that made it, and each run adds a hash-chained entry, so an edit made later shows up. To check, run python -m tracking verify from core/.

    Common questions

    Straight answers, including what QUANTA does not do.

    Is this investment advice?

    No. QUANTA is a research tool for education and information. It doesn't know your goals, taxes or full finances. Check every price with your broker before you act.

    Can QUANTA trade in my account?

    No. My Portfolio reads your Robinhood account and nothing else. Order tools are blocked in code before any request is sent, and order execution is switched off in the source. Every trade is yours to place.

    Do I need to be an experienced investor?

    No. Each page leads with a plain answer, and the reasons and numbers sit one step away for when you want them. The examples on this page run on the same rules as the real pages, so they are a good place to learn how QUANTA thinks.

    Does QUANTA cost anything?

    No. QUANTA is free, open-source software under the MIT License. It runs on your computer with free public data; keys for other data providers are optional.

    Why is a stock that Research rates a Buy missing from Picks?

    Picks is stricter. A scan also needs reward/risk of (Research needs ), a risk grade of A to C, a score above the scan's floor, enough conviction, a return that covers costs, room in its sector, and a second full Research check.

    Why does My Portfolio say Do Not Add to a stock that Research likes?

    Because of the account, not the stock. New money stops when one stock reaches of the account, when its sector or a group of holdings that move together reaches , or when an earnings report is due within days. The view on the stock stays the same.

    What does "Model not yet validated" mean?

    The scoring model behind the call hasn't yet passed tests on past data. "Tested: no edge found" goes further: the model was tested and showed no reliable edge. Treat either kind of call as a research idea, and position sizes as a risk budget, not a prediction.

    How does it decide how many shares to buy?

    From the loss at the stop: of capital ( for a Strong Buy), scaled by the call and the score, divided by the distance to the stop. Limits then trim it: at most in one stock, the risk grade, and the mandate, which funds for Conservative. Shares are whole, with a minimum trade of of capital, at least and at most .

    Which markets and assets does it cover?

    US stocks and ETFs, and Indian stocks on the NSE (add .NS) or BSE (add .BO) in Research. Scans cover ready-made US and NSE universes, or up to tickers of your own. My Portfolio analyses stocks and ETFs, not options or crypto.

    What doesn't QUANTA do?

    It has no price alerts, saved watchlists, report downloads or side-by-side stock comparison. Picks can copy its picks as a TradingView watchlist, and a scan's error log can be downloaded.

    Why can a scan take several minutes?

    QUANTA spaces out its data requests so free providers don't block it. A quick scan takes a few minutes; a full one takes longer.
