CAVEATS
- Static list of present-day large caps: absolute returns carry survivorship and selection bias. Read each portfolio against the equal-weight benchmark of the same names.
- The live engine's gates, stops, sizes and corporate-event checks are not simulated. This measures the ranking, not the calls.
- Sentiment and analyst inputs are neutral in the panel (no point-in-time source).
- Drawdown is measured at rebalance dates and understates the worst fall inside a period.
- With sentiment neutral at 50 the panel's scores sit closer to 50 than live scores do, so the band table tests the live cut-offs on a compressed scale (few rows reach 75).
- A portfolio that needs a year of history (12-1 momentum) starts later, so its benchmark figure covers a slightly shorter period than the others.
- One fixed configuration was run. Its result is a description of this sample, not a validated strategy, and it does not change any evidence status.
