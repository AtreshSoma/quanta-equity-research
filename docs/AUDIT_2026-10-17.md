## Measured, not changed — owner decision needed

**#4 Scanner target horizon.** The scanner's pre-filter target averages
structure with the 12-month analyst consensus, while its stop is a few ATR.
Pro-rating the consensus to the 63-session Research horizon was implemented and
measured on 50 liquid US names (live yfinance, 17 Oct): names clearing the 1.8
scanner gate fell from **15 to 1**, median R:R 1.35 → 0.94 — the book would be
empty on almost every scan. It was reverted. What users see is already honest:
published levels are the Research engine's (target pro-rated, must clear 1.50
since #5). Options: (a) keep as is; (b) pro-rate and lower `RR.SCANNER_MIN`
toward `RR.RESEARCH_MIN`; (c) pro-rate and accept a mostly empty book.
PRODUCT.md marks funding thresholds as owner decisions.

**#15 Size scales with score** (`SIZING.SCORE_MULT_*`, `SIGNAL_SIZE_MULT`)
although `elite_pro.composite` is `UNTESTED`. Flat risk per trade until the
ledger shows the score is calibrated is the conservative alternative.
