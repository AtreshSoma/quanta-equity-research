## Decided (follow-up, same day)

**#4 Scanner target horizon: kept, closed.** The scanner's pre-filter target
averages structure with the 12-month analyst consensus. Pro-rating that
consensus to the 63-session Research horizon was measured on 50 liquid US names
(live yfinance, 17 Oct): names clearing the 1.8 scanner gate fell from **15 to
1**, median R:R 1.35 to 0.94, an empty book on almost every scan. The scanner
figure only pre-selects names for the Research check; the PUBLISHED levels are
the Research engine's (target pro-rated to its horizon) and must clear 1.50
before a pick is funded (#5). Lowering `RR.SCANNER_MIN` instead would loosen a
funding threshold, which PRODUCT.md rules out.

**#15 Score-scaled sizing: removed.** `SIZING.SCORE_MULT_BASE/SPAN` are 1.0/0.0
while `elite_pro.composite` is `UNTESTED`: size no longer tilts with an
unvalidated score. The call tier (Buy, Accumulate) still sets size. Restore
0.7/0.5 once the ledger shows the score is calibrated.

**#20 Entry at the last close: closed.** Each pick publishes an entry zone, the
close and session its levels were built on, and the page tells the reader that
prices may have moved since the scan.
