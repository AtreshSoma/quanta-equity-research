CAVEATS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Fundamental, valuation and quality legs are rebuilt from SEC XBRL filings, using only facts filed before each date (apex_quant/pit_fundamentals.py) and the live analyzers' own scoring code. Sentiment (news, insider flow) and analyst targets have no point-in-time source and are held neutral, so the DCF runs without an analyst anchor.
2. Rebuilt figures follow stated definitions (TTM from discrete quarters, diluted shares, debt excluding leases) that can differ from Yahoo's own fields by a few percent.
3. Universe is a static present-day list: results carry survivorship and selection bias. Absolute numbers are optimistic; relative comparisons are the trustworthy part.
4. t_stat is computed on non-overlapping dates. t_stat_overlapping is shown only to expose the inflation from overlapping forward returns and must not be quoted.
5. IC statistics exclude transaction costs. Costs enter the walk-forward trade simulation, which is a separate report.
